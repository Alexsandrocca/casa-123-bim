# Decisions log

- 2026-09-29 — Web app (Vite + React + TypeScript + three.js) instead of Blender or Revit, so the family can edit it in a browser. Professionals get the model through the IFC export (spec 07).
- 2026-09-29 — Version 2 is the starting design. Version 1 is kept for comparison.
- 2026-09-29 — Rooms are stored as rectangles ("cells"), exactly like the prototype, so the areas match it to the cent and wall dragging behaves the same. Walls are real model elements that are rebuilt from the rooms after every room edit; wall ids, thickness and doors survive the rebuild.
- 2026-09-29 — Wall centrelines sit on the room edges, so room areas are the prototype's areas (centreline areas). Net areas inside finishes can come later if the manager wants them.
- 2026-09-29 — Retaining wall thickness 0.25 m (the brief gives none).
- 2026-09-29 — Buried part of the lower-level side walls: south wall y 8.5–10.5, north wall y 8.5–8.9. Reason: the laundry window starts at 10.8 on the south side and the studio window at 9.0 on the north side, and a window cannot sit in a retaining wall. Stored in the model (`site.cut`) so it can change. See QUESTIONS.
- 2026-09-29 — Lot shape: the south boundary is square to the street and the rear is 1 m wider on the north side (14 m front, 15 m rear). Used by the Civil Code 1.301 and setback checks. See QUESTIONS.
- 2026-09-29 — Only glass that the prototype counts as windows counts for the 1/8 window ratio; glass sliding doors do not. Same result as the prototype.
- 2026-09-29 — Civil Code 1.301 check covers windows and glass sliders against the side and rear boundaries, not the street.
- 2026-09-29 — Minimum areas follow the prototype's table: master bedroom 10 m², other bedrooms 8 m², bath/WC 2.5 m², kitchen 4 m², living and work rooms 8 m², closet none.
- 2026-09-29 — Version 1 stairs are modelled as U-stairs (two flights of 9 + 8 risers, 1.15 m wide, landing at the front), from the prototype drawing. Version 2 stairs come from plan-v2.json (17 risers of 0.182, treads 0.27, 1.5 m wide).
- 2026-09-29 — Every edit is a pure command; each version keeps its own undo history (100 steps). A drag is one undo step.
- 2026-09-29 — Added "Add door" and "Add window" tools to the toolbar, because double-click does not work well on a tablet.
- 2026-09-29 — Renaming a room to the name of another room on the same floor is refused: the two would merge and their wall would disappear.
- 2026-09-29 — Each floor fills the plan view (as in the prototype). A fixed frame for all floors was tried and made the lower level too small on a tablet.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — The 3D view is built from simple boxes and ground polygons computed from the model by a pure function (`src/scene/build3d.ts`). It keeps no copy of the geometry and is unit-tested without WebGL. Holes for doors and windows are made by splitting walls into pieces (no boolean operations): exact for rectangular openings and fast.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — Structure is generated once from the grid and the slabs and stored in the model, so each column and beam has an id and editable properties. Version 2 grid: x 0 / 3.2 / 8.6, y 0 / 5 / 8.5 / 12.6 / 15 (the 8.2 line from plan-v2 is only a stair edge, so it gets a trimmer beam, not a column row).
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — "0.40 m total depth" read as structure zone: 0.14 m steel-deck slab on 0.26 m beams (W250×32.7). The headroom check still uses 0.40 m.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — New slabs: the roof of the lower level under the veranda (+0.60) and the garage roof (+3.70, parapet 0.30). Roof eaves 0.40 m by default (the city limit is 0.70 m).
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — Ground model: natural plane 0.00 at the street to −2.00 at the rear; garden −2.55 behind the cut (y 8.5); north ramp starts at the street at 0.00 and falls 12.5 % until it meets the garden (at y ≈ 16.4, just past the house); south passage falls evenly from the street to the garden at y 10.5 (where the south retaining wall ends). Driveway rises from 0.00 to the garage floor +0.10.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — Street-level outer walls go down to +0.10, and a concrete plinth closes the crawlspace down to the ground. Walls around the garage start at the garage floor (+0.10).
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — Where a stair flight passes over a wall (the down flight crosses the cut line at y 8.5), the wall is automatically notched below the flight. Without this the stair would hit the retaining wall. See Q4.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — Support ("nothing floats") rule: footings below the ground; columns on a footing; beams need one supported contact and two contacts in all (columns or other beams); suspended slabs need two supported beams; slabs on grade may sit on up to 1.20 m of compacted fill; walls, stairs and decks must rest on a supported slab, beam, strip footing, wall or the ground. A plausibility rule, not a structural analysis.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — Guards 1.10 m are generated where a floor, landing or stair edge drops more than 0.50 m and no wall covers it from the floor to 0.90 m.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — The vertical section is dragged by a red bar on its top edge (dragging the whole plane would block clicks on the building behind it). A slider does the same.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — Version 1 U-stair adjusted so the top flight lands exactly on the upper floor edge (flights 9 + 8 risers; the first flight starts 0.27 m into the hall).
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02) — Walk mode ignores door leaves (you can walk through a door opening even when doors are shown closed), so the walk never gets stuck on a closed door.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02b) — Version 3 is generated from Version 2 by the importer (`deriveV3` + `addV3Front`), so Version 2 stays untouched and Version 3 can be regenerated. It opens by default; the saved "last version" setting was reset once so the family lands on Version 3.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02b) — Floors that do not fill their outline rectangle (the Version 3 street level is L-shaped) get their outer walls from the rooms' outer edge, and their floor slab follows the rooms. Rectangular floors keep the old method, so Versions 1 and 2 did not change at all.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02b) — A run of room edges that touches the outside anywhere is treated as an outer wall and cannot be dragged (same rule as the prototype's fixed outline).
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02b) — Street-level doors and windows moved with their rooms: WC door y 3.1–3.8, storage door y 4.3–5.1, WC high window y 3.0–4.0; pantry door and window stay. New: kitchen window x 5.6–8.2 and glazed service door x 3.5–4.4 on y 5.0; passage window y 3.2–4.4 on x 3.2.
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02b) — The patio is a Deck at +0.60 on a slab of compacted fill (1.20 m deep, so it reads as a podium and passes the support rule), with a planter strip on the north edge (x 7.9–8.6) and three risers down to the carport at x 3.5–4.7. Its north edge gets the automatic 1.10 m guard (drop to the ramp).
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02b) — Carport frame: 4 HSS 100×100 columns, 2 W150 beams (front and rear), 0.10 m insulated sandwich roof, 0.60 m pad footings. Roof top +2.70 at the street, +2.95 at the house (5 %). Clear height under the front beam 2.46 m. Bays x 2.75–5.25 and 5.85–8.35 (0.60 m between them for the doors).
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02b) — Parking check: each bay ≥ 2.50 × 5.00, inside the carport, no overlap, and a free 0.60 m strip on at least one side (a strip between two bays serves both).
- 2026-09-29 (spec 02b) — New check status "to confirm" for rules only an authority can decide. It is never counted as a pass. Used for "Carport in the front setback".
